/production  (jobs list)

/production/new  (create job)

- Fields: meters_sent (required), assign to (dropdown of workshop-role users, optional), notes

/production/[id]  (job detail + completion)
- Show job info: product, fabric, meters_sent, status, assigned to, notes, date

    * leftover_meters_returned field (numeric, must be >= 0 and <= meters_sent)
    * one row per product variant to enter pieces_produced (integer >= 0)
    * at least 1 total piece required
- ON COMPLETE (use db.batch() — no WebSocket):
    1. fabrics.stock_meters += leftover_meters_returned
    2. fabric_consumed = meters_sent - leftover_meters_returned
    3. snapshot fabric_avg = fabric.avg_cost_per_meter at this moment
    4. total_pieces = sum of all pieces_produced

         - update variant: finished_stock_qty = new_qty, avg_finished_cost = new_avg_cost
         - insert production_job_outputs row
    7. update job: status='completed', leftover_meters_returned, fabric_avg_cost_at_completion, fabric_consumed_meters, cost_per_piece, completed_at=now()
