- Do NOT hardcode colors, fonts, or spacing values in components. Ever.

fabric_purchases

- supplier (nullable)
- purchase_date (date)
- note (nullable)
- created_at

PURCHASE LOGIC (important):
When a fabric_purchase is added:
- fabrics.stock_meters += meters
- recompute fabrics.avg_cost_per_meter as a WEIGHTED AVERAGE:
  new_avg = ((old_stock * old_avg) + (meters * cost_per_meter)) / (old_stock + meters)
(This weighted average will be used for accurate COGS in a later phase — get it right now.)
