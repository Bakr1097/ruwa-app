CALCULATIONS (critical — get the accounting right):
- Revenue = sum of total_revenue from sales where order_status != 'returned' and sale_date in range
- COGS = sum of total_cogs from sales where order_status != 'returned' and sale_date in range
- Gross Profit = Revenue - COGS
- Gross Margin % = Gross Profit / Revenue * 100 (guard divide-by-zero -> show 0)
- Operating Expenses = sum of expenses.amount where expense_date in range
- Net Profit = Gross Profit - Operating Expenses
- Net Margin % = Net Profit / Revenue * 100 (guard divide-by-zero)
RETURNED SALES are excluded from Revenue and COGS (order_status != 'returned'). This matches the Phase 3 rule.
